Everything You Need To Build Healthy Money Habits

Designed for students and young professionals who want clean, transparent personal accounting without clutter.

💳

Expense Tracking

Record everyday transactions with categories, dates, payment modes, and descriptions. Instantly filter, search, edit, or delete logged expenses.

📊

Budget Monitoring

Allocate monthly allowances across food, travel, education, and utilities. Real-time indicators alert you before category limits are breached.

🎯

Savings Goals

Set targets for emergency funds or new gadgets. Watch live progress bars grow and receive congratulatory milestones when targets are achieved.

⏰

Bill Reminders

Never miss an EMI, broadband, rent, or electricity bill again. Color-coded date calculations automatically flag upcoming and overdue payments.
